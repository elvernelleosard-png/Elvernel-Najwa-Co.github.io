Remedies & Strategy

How We Can Help in a SaaS Dispute

When cloud providers default or enterprise software implementations fail, corporate clients face distinct strategic crossroads. We offer three tailored courses of action:

Option 01 • High Court Action

Litigation

When software vendors commit repudiatory breach or cause catastrophic downtime, we institute formal proceedings in the High Court of Malaya.

  • ⚖ High Court Writ of Summons: Formal filing in the Commercial Division for urgent judicial relief.
  • ⚖ Contracts Act 1950 (s.74 & s.75): Claiming direct and consequential losses, and enforcing stipulated liquidated damages.
  • ⚖ Liquidated Damages Enforcement: Applying Federal Court precedent (Cubic Electronics) to enforce contractually agreed sums.
  • ⚖ Authoritative Case Precedents: Marshalling Commonwealth and Malaysian authorities to secure judgments and costs.
Assess Litigation Options
Option 02 • Without-Prejudice ADR

Settlement

A strategic, confidential mechanism to recover capital, secure refunds, and preserve ongoing business relationships without courtroom exposure.

  • 🤝 Refund & Partial Compensation: Structured without-prejudice negotiations for direct cash restitution and cost mitigation.
  • 🤝 Heads of Agreement (HoA): Drafting binding preliminary commercial terms and formal settlement deeds.
  • 🤝 Faster & Cost-Efficient: Bypassing lengthy court hearing schedules, appeals, and escalating trial expenses.
  • 🤝 Commercial Goodwill: Resolving acute friction while keeping vital supply chain or integration partners viable.
Explore Settlement Terms
Option 03 • Commercial Restructuring

Renegotiation

Revising the foundational terms of an ongoing SaaS agreement where transitioning to an alternate vendor is unfeasible or cost-prohibitive.

  • 📝 Amending Key Terms: Restructuring subscription fees, tier billing, and milestone-linked payment schedules.
  • 📝 Adjusting Liability Caps: Re-negotiating aggregate liability ceilings to protect against catastrophic system failures.
  • 📝 Service Credit Enforcements: Imposing aggressive, automated SLA penalty credits for recurring service interruptions.
  • 📝 Risk Reduction: Maintaining operational continuity while legally fortifying client contractual defenses.
